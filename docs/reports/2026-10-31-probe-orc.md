## The fields it got wrong

12 of 78. The model's reasons are in [wrong.md](2026-10-31-probe-orc/wrong.md), and every answer is in `answers.json`.

- **Circles missed (5):** J1313+5003, J1436+4832, J1608+6123, J0847+7022, J0320+1610.
- **Others called ring-like (7):** Arp 143, NGC 7217, NGC 4736, NGC 6720 (M57), NGC 3587 (M97), NGC 6543, SNR G043.3-00.2.

I did not view any field again for this report. Item 3 had already described J1436+4832 as a large irregular complex, and the model's reason calls it irregular too. It also predicted that M57, M97 and NGC 4736 might be called ring-like, because they are round rings or discs of about the right size in these images, and all three were.

## How to read the 53 of 60

The random fields are easy, and the model called all 30 not ring-like. On the 30 lookalikes alone it was right on 23 (0.767), which would be under 80 percent if the bar were set on them alone. The bar as written counts all 60, so this does not change the verdict, but the hard test here is the lookalikes, and it let about one in four through.

## Recognition check

The model named 1 of the 3 fields, and named it correctly: NGC 6720 as "Ring Nebula", with the reason "consistent with M57". So at least one famous object is known by its shape from these images. It costs nothing on the score, because that field is labelled not ring-like and was still called ring-like, which counts as wrong.

The other two count as not recognised, but their reasons are poor evidence. On NGC 4736 the reason is gibberish, and on 3C 196 it reads "Image is not legible/vision is unavailable". Neither call failed, and both carried both images, but I cannot say what the model made of them. The check did not cover any of the circles, so whether it knows any of them is untested.

## Weaknesses that bear on the verdict

- **The prompt was tuned on the circles, including the held-out ones.** Item 3 reworded it after viewing them, before any model saw it. The 7 of 9 on the held-out circles is therefore not an independent test, and the 13 may be flattered.
- **One field from the bar on one run.** With 13 of 18 the interval runs from 0.49 to 0.875, which includes the 14 of 18 (0.78) the bar asks for. This run neither shows the model can reach the bar nor rules it out. Run-to-run variation was not measured, because each field was asked once at the fixed low effort.
- **The labels are not all clean.** The circle list holds candidates as well as confirmed circles, and item 3 found J1150+2449 and J1222+6436 barely above the noise. Several lookalikes do look like the prompt's description.
- **One empty reason.** Arp 143 has a blank reason, and its answer counts as given.
